About Caverra Empowerment Foundation

Inspiring Purpose • Building Confidence • Shaping Futures

Our Story

Caverra Empowerment Foundation (CEF), formerly known as Caverra World Foundation, was born from a deep passion to uplift and empower Nigerian teenagers and youths. Witnessing the immense potential and unique challenges faced by young people—poverty, lack of parental guidance, peer pressure, limited opportunities, teenage pregnancy, substance abuse, and juvenile delinquency—our founder envisioned an organization dedicated to providing the guidance, resources, and inspiration necessary for them to truly thrive.

Since its inception, CEF has embarked on a remarkable journey, touching the lives of thousands through innovative programs, impactful events, and unwavering mentorship. We believe in fostering environments where young minds can freely explore their talents, build essential life skills, and cultivate a strong sense of purpose.

Our story is one of consistent growth, fueled by the unwavering belief in the power of every teenager to Discover, Maximize, and Excel. From grassroots initiatives to nationally recognized events like National Teenagers' Day, we've grown into a beacon of hope and transformation.

Inspired by the Nigerian proverb, "It takes a whole village to raise a child," CEF emphasizes collective effort in empowering the next generation through collaboration among families, schools, organizations, communities, and government entities.

Why We Exist

The teenage years represent a time of immense growth, exploration, and self-discovery, yet many young people encounter significant barriers that can lead to detrimental outcomes. Challenges like poverty, inadequate guidance, peer pressure, and restricted access to resources often result in issues such as teenage pregnancy, substance abuse, school dropouts, and involvement in juvenile delinquency or cybercrime.

CEF exists to counteract these challenges by offering positive role models, mentorship, and a robust support system. We believe that empowered teenagers evolve into empowered adults who drive positive change.

Through carefully designed programs, CEF equips teens with essential life skills, builds their resilience, and cultivates a healthy sense of self-worth, ensuring every young person has the opportunity to thrive regardless of their background.

Meet Our Founder

Mrs. Roseline Ify Anijekwu

Founder & CEO, Caverra Empowerment Foundation

Teen Coach & Author

Lagos State Champion for OCI Foundation

Mrs. Roseline Ify Anijekwu is a passionate advocate for youth development and empowerment. As a dedicated teen coach and author, she recognized the critical need for structured guidance and support systems that cater specifically to the unique challenges and opportunities faced by young people in Nigeria.

With years of experience working directly with teenagers, Mrs. Anijekwu founded Caverra Empowerment Foundation (formerly Caverra World Foundation) to create lasting impact in the lives of Nigerian youths. Her approach combines mentorship, education, and motivation to foster personal growth, resilience, and purposeful living among teenagers.

Her dedication extends beyond CEF, as evidenced by her role as the Lagos State Champion for the OCI Foundation. In this capacity, she actively promotes initiatives like the Arm Our Youths (ArOY) Health Campaign, integrating vital health education with youth empowerment.

Mrs. Anijekwu's vision and tireless efforts are the driving force behind CEF's success in shaping the lives of countless Nigerian teenagers. She believes that every teenager holds immense potential waiting to be unleashed, and through Caverra Empowerment Foundation, she provides the roadmap for them to discover, maximize, and excel.
