Our Programs & Events

National Teenagers' Day Celebration

National Teenagers' Day is Caverra Empowerment Foundation's flagship annual event dedicated to celebrating, inspiring, and empowering teenagers across Nigeria. This vibrant celebration serves as a powerful platform to recognize the unique potential of young people, providing them with invaluable insights, mentorship, and a profound sense of community and belonging.

History & Milestones

The inaugural event took place in 2018, marking the beginning of an annual tradition that has grown exponentially. Each year from 2018 through 2025, we've celebrated this momentous occasion with increasing impact and recognition.

2025 Milestone Event

On March 20, 2025, we held our most significant celebration yet in Festac Town, Lagos. This event was recognized by the press as pioneering in uniquely commemorating National Teenager Day. Highlights included:

  • Award presentation to Vice Admiral AZ Gambo (former Chief of Naval Staff, received by representatives)
  • Media coverage by Nigeria Television Authority (NTA) and The Sun Newspapers
  • Partnership with OCI Foundation and other key stakeholders
  • Record attendance and community engagement

Event Features

  • Energetic Performances: Music and dance displays by talented teenage performers showcasing their creativity and skills.
  • Awards & Recognition: Ceremonies honoring outstanding achievements, leadership, and community service among teenagers.
  • Cultural Presentations: Showcasing Nigeria's rich heritage through traditional dances, music, and attire.
  • Speak-Out Sessions: Platforms for self-expression and public speaking skill development.
  • NDLEA Talk: Educational sessions on substance abuse dangers and healthy choices, in partnership with the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency.

Annual Teens Camp

Our Annual Teens Camp is a transformative experience organized in partnership with Caverra Youth and HFCC Youth. This camp provides a supportive, interactive environment for skill development, relationship building, and passion exploration in a fun, engaging setting.

Key Benefits

Personal Growth and Development

Self-reflection, goal-setting, and character building activities that foster confidence and resilience.

Leadership Skills and Teamwork

Activities and challenges designed to enhance leadership abilities, collaboration, and peer relationships.

Mentorship and Guidance

Insights from experienced facilitators to help navigate adolescence and make informed future decisions.

Networking and Community Building

Connections with diverse peers, fostering a sense of belonging and social responsibility.

Skill Acquisition and Entrepreneurship

Platforms to learn new skills, explore passions, and develop entrepreneurial mindsets.

Fun and Relaxation

Safe, enjoyable activities promoting overall well-being and happiness.

Purpose

  • Empowerment and Capacity Building: Equip teens with necessary skills and attitudes for success

  • Self-Discovery: Deepen self-understanding and broaden world perspective

Preparatory Seminar for University Life

A comprehensive orientation program for teenagers transitioning to university life, addressing unrealistic expectations and preparing them for academic and personal responsibilities.

Five Areas of Preparedness

Physical Preparedness

Healthy habits and routines for academic and personal success.

Social Preparedness

Building relationships, networking, and navigating social dynamics.

Emotional Preparedness

Managing stress, anxiety, and maintaining mental well-being.

Academic Preparedness

Study skills, goal-setting, and understanding academic expectations.

Spiritual Preparedness

Cultivating purpose, values, and personal identity.

Participants gain a competitive edge for seamless transitions into university life.

Orphanage Visits

A cherished tradition at Caverra Empowerment Foundation, our orphanage visits—supported by generous sponsors—create profound impacts on both the teenagers who visit and the children they meet.

Impact on Participating Teens

  • Reality Check: Broadens perspectives by exposing teens to different life circumstances and challenges.
  • Gratitude & Empathy: Inspires gratitude toward God and parents while fostering empathy and compassion.
  • Motivation & Drive: Encourages teens to become more driven, supportive, and socially responsible.
  • Character Development: Develops understanding of kindness, compassion, and generosity.

By interacting with orphaned children, our teenagers gain valuable life lessons about resilience, appreciation, and the importance of giving back to society.
