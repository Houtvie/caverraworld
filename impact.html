Our Journey: Milestones & Achievements

2018 - The Beginning

Inaugural National Teenagers' Day celebration launched, establishing the foundation for youth empowerment in Nigeria.

2019-2023 - Growth & Expansion

Annual National Teenagers' Day celebrations continued with increasing participation. Introduction of Teens Camps, Monthly Hangouts, and Preparatory Seminars. Thousands of teens gained confidence, discovered purpose, and transformed their lives.

2024 - Recognition & Innovation

Inaugural National Teenager Day recognized by the press as groundbreaking. Teens Camp emphasized personal growth, leadership, and fun. Preparatory Seminars equipped students for university transitions. Orphanage visits inspired empathy and social responsibility.

2025 - Historic Milestone

March 20, 2025: National Teenagers' Day held in Festac Town, Lagos, marking our most significant celebration yet:

  • Award presentation to Vice Admiral AZ Gambo (former Chief of Naval Staff)
  • Media coverage by Nigeria Television Authority (NTA) and The Sun Newspapers
  • Strengthened partnerships with OCI Foundation and NDLEA
  • Emphasis on positive orientation and self-discovery for teenagers
  • Recognized as pioneers in uniquely commemorating National Teenager Day

Success Stories & Testimonials

David Emeka: From Shy to Scholar

"Before Caverra Empowerment Foundation, I was very shy and struggled with public speaking. Attending their workshops and the National Teenagers' Day changed everything. The confidence I gained helped me excel in school and even win a debate competition. I now believe in myself more than ever!"

- David Emeka, University Student

Aisha Bello: Discovering Her Creative Path

"The Teens' Hangout sessions opened my eyes to so many possibilities. I met mentors who encouraged my artistic passion and helped me understand how I could pursue a career in design. CEF didn't just inspire me; it gave me direction and purpose."

- Aisha Bello, Aspiring Graphic Designer

Samuel Okoro: A Voice for His Community

"The leadership training at Caverra Empowerment Foundation was invaluable. I learned how to organize, communicate effectively, and truly make a difference. I've since started a community project to educate younger kids on environmental awareness, all thanks to CEF."

- Samuel Okoro, Community Youth Leader
